Download for Mac Apple Silicon · .dmg

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 14 Sonoma or later, and Ableton Live 11 or 12.

Getting started

  1. Open the downloaded Palette.dmg and drag PaletteBar into your Applications folder.
  2. Because Palette is in early alpha, macOS will say the app "can't be opened" the first time. To allow it, open Terminal (Applications → Utilities → Terminal), paste this command, and press Return:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PaletteBar.app
  3. Open PaletteBar from your Applications folder. You'll only need to do step 2 once.
  4. On first launch Palette asks for your Anthropic API key, then walks you through enabling the Ableton control surface. Have Ableton Live open to finish setup.
This one-time Terminal step goes away once Palette ships a notarized build.
